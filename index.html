PRJ-02

Furuta Pendulum: Swing-Up & LQR

Underactuated Robotics
2 people · Oct – Dec 2025

A rotary inverted pendulum has one motor for two degrees of freedom. A hybrid controller pumps energy into the pendulum to swing it up, then hands over to an LQR that holds it upright.

What I did

  • Derived the full nonlinear model with my teammate using the Euler–Lagrange method, writing it in manipulator form and using the cyclic coordinate to reduce its order.
  • Designed the energy-pumping swing-up law and the LQR stabilizer, with a switch between them near the upright position.
  • Proved stability with Lyapunov analysis for both phases and validated the whole system in MATLAB/Simulink and Simscape Multibody, including disturbance tests.

Key numbers

LQR weightsQ = diag(300, 15, 3), R = 2
Optimal gainK = [41.556 −2.739 4.124]
Swing-up lawu = k·ΔE·θ̇₂·cos θ₂
Arm / pendulum mass0.12 kg / 0.15 kg

Mechatronics System Design II

PRJ-04

Hypoglycemia Detection from ECG

Deep Learning
Team of 3 · Oct – Dec 2025

Can a 10-second ECG segment reveal low blood sugar? Only about 5% of segments were hypoglycemic, so the hard part was the imbalance.

What I did

  • Designed and trained a 1D CNN + BiLSTM classifier on raw 2,500-sample ECG segments.
  • Trained a GAN to generate synthetic hypoglycemic segments, raising the positives from 1,723 to about 5,169.
  • Tested alternatives (a feature-based MLP, TimeGAN, patient-wise splits) and tuned the decision threshold for the best F1.

Key numbers

F1 (hypoglycemia)0.781
AUROC0.981
Recall / precision0.759 / 0.805
Model size180,161 parameters

Results use a stratified random split. Patient-wise splits were tested but left too few positive events to learn from.
